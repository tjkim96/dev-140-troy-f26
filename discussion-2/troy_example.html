How Sourdough Is Made

Here's brief overview of how to make sourdough.

First, you mix flour and water and a starter.

Then, you let it ferment for a while.

After it ferments and rises, you shape it into a loaf.

You bake it until it's golden brown.

Finally, slice and enjoy with butter!
